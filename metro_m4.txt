M4 document: Metro Istanbul line M4, Kadıköy - Sabiha Gökçen Airport Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M4 line identifier: M4 Kadıköy - Sabiha Gökçen Airport Metro Line.
M4 operator: The operator of the M4 line is Metro Istanbul.
M4 description: The M4 line spans 33.5 km connecting Kadıköy to Sabiha Gökçen Airport across 23 underground stations, following the D100 highway corridor through Acıbadem and Tavşantepe.
M4 description: Phase 1 from Kadıköy to Kartal opened in 2012, Phase 2 extended the line to Tavşantepe in 2016, and Phase 3 extended it to Sabiha Gökçen Airport in 2022.
M4 line length metric: Total line length is 33.5 km.
M4 total stations metric: Total stations on M4 route is 23 stations.
M4 fleet size metric: Active fleet size is 144 cars running as 36 4-car train sets.
M4 travel duration metric: One-way journey duration is 52 minutes.
M4 operating hours metric: Operating hours are 06:00-00:00.
M4 passenger capacity metric: Daily passenger capacity or ridership is 300,000 passengers.
M4 frequency metric: Peak hour travel frequency is every 5 minutes.
M4 depot location metric: Maintenance facilities located in the Maltepe Station area feature a 52-car depot and 32-car workshop that are 100% underground.
M4 full station list: The M4 line spans 23 stations from start terminus 01-Kadıköy, 02-Ayrılık Çeşmesi, 03-Acıbadem, 04-Ünalan, 05-Göztepe, 06-Yenisahra, 07-Kozyatağı, 08-Bostancı (features an additional track line and two platforms), 09-Küçükyalı, 10-Maltepe, 11-Huzurevi, 12-Gülsuyu, 13-Esenkent, 14-Hastane-Adliye, 15-Soğanlık, 16-Kartal, 17-Yakacık-Adnan Kahveci, 18-Pendik, 19-Tavşantepe, 20-Fevzi Çakmak-Hastane, 21-Yayalar-Şeyhli, 22-Kurtköy, to end terminus 23-Sabiha Gökçen Havalimanı.
M4 engineering construction: TBM was used for Kadıköy-Kozyatağı and Kartal-Kaynarca sections, while NATM was used for Kozyatağı-Kartal section.
M4 engineering structure: The entire line, including depot and workshop, is 100% underground.
M4 platform specifications: Station platforms are 180 meters long to support 8-car train operations, and Bostancı Station features a 3rd track and 2 platforms.
M4 signaling system: Thales CBTC signalisation system with Movement Block continuous communication allows automated driverless train parking.
M4 safety features: Fire and evacuation systems are fully NFPA-compliant with smoke control using ultra-heat-resistant, non-toxic materials.
M4 power backup: Power supply is fed through 3 independent grid terminals with dual-side generators auto-starting within 15 seconds during total blackout and UPS backups sustaining control for 3 hours.
M4 security features: Security infrastructure includes 1,281 CCTV cameras monitored 24/7 along with uniformed security personnel.
M4 commissioning tender: Date of Tender occurred on 14.01.2008.
M4 commissioning contract: Contract Date occurred on 06.03.2008.
M4 commissioning commencement: Commencement Date occurred on 21.03.2008.
M4 commissioning train arrival: Arrival of the first train at the depot occurred on 11.01.2011.
M4 commissioning track laying: Laying the first train on the tracks occurred on 27.01.2011.
M4 commissioning tunnel completion: Completion of Tunnels occurred in October 2011.
M4 commissioning signaling: Commissioning of signalisation systems occurred in March 2012.
M4 commissioning test runs: Start of test runs occurred on 08.05.2012.
M4 commissioning phase 1: Phase 1 commissioning between Kadıköy and Kartal occurred on 17.08.2012.
M4 commissioning Ayrılık Çeşmesi: Opening of Ayrılık Çeşmesi Station occurred on 29.10.2013.
M4 commissioning phase 2: Phase 2 commissioning of extension to Tavşantepe occurred on 10.10.2016.
M4 commissioning phase 3: Phase 3 commissioning of extension to Sabiha Gökçen Airport occurred on 02.10.2022.
M4 transfer Kadıköy Station: Start terminus Kadıköy Station offers transfer connections to T3 Kadıköy - Moda Tram Line and ferries/sea buses/motorboats.
M4 transfer Ayrılık Çeşmesi Station: Ayrılık Çeşmesi Station offers transfer connections to B1 Marmaray Line.
M4 transfer Ünalan Station: Ünalan Station offers transfer connections to Metrobus (BRT) Line.
M4 transfer Kozyatağı Station: Kozyatağı Station offers transfer connections to M8 Bostancı - Parseller Metro Line.
M4 transfer Pendik Station: Pendik Station offers transfer connections to Pendik High Speed Train (YHT) Station via İETT bus connections.
