M2 document: Metro Istanbul line M2, Yenikapı - Seyrantepe - Hacıosman Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M2 line identifier: M2 Yenikapı - Seyrantepe - Hacıosman Metro Line.
M2 description: Construction for the M2 line began in 1992, with the first operational stage commissioned on September 16, 2000.
M2 description: The mainline serves the route from Yenikapı to Hacıosman, while a dedicated branch line operates from Sanayi Mahallesi Station to Seyrantepe requiring a transfer at Sanayi Mahallesi Station.
M2 network length metric: Total network length is 23.49 km.
M2 total stations metric: Total stations on M2 route is 16 stations.
M2 fleet size metric: Active fleet size is 192 vehicles.
M2 travel duration metric: One-way journey duration is 32 minutes.
M2 operating hours metric: Operating hours are 06:00-00:00.
M2 passenger capacity metric: Daily passenger capacity averages 500,000 passengers.
M2 daily journeys metric: Daily journeys total 219 one-way journeys, with 694 total daily journeys.
M2 frequency metric: Mainline frequency between Yenikapı and Hacıosman is every 3 minutes 55 seconds during 8-car operation on weekdays and Saturdays.
M2 frequency metric: Branch frequency between Sanayi Mahallesi and Seyrantepe is every 8.5 minutes all day.
M2 full station list: The M2 mainline spans 15 stations from start terminus 01-Yenikapı, 02-Vezneciler - İstanbul Üniversitesi, 03-Haliç, 04-Şişhane, 05-Taksim, 06-Osmanbey, 07-Şişli - Mecidiyeköy, 08-Gayrettepe, 09-Levent, 10-4.Levent, 11-Sanayi Mahallesi, 12-İTÜ - Ayazağa, 13-Atatürk Oto Sanayi, 14-Darüşşafaka, to end terminus 15-Hacıosman.
M2 branch station: Seyrantepe is a branch station accessible via shuttle transfer at Sanayi Mahallesi.
M2 viaduct station: Haliç Station is a bridge and viaduct station built over the Haliç Metro Bridge.
M2 tunnel underground stations: All other 15 remaining stations on the M2 route are built as tunnel and underground stations.
M2 commissioning groundbreaking: Groundbreaking occurred on 19.08.1992.
M2 commissioning tunnel merger: Merger of Taksim - Şişli Tunnels occurred on 12.06.1994.
M2 commissioning tunnel merger: Merger of Şişli - 4. Levent Tunnels occurred on 08.07.1994.
M2 commissioning tunnel merger: Merger of Taksim - Şişli and 4. Levent Tunnels occurred on 30.04.1995.
M2 commissioning fleet launch: Launching of the vehicles into the tunnel occurred on 11.01.1999.
M2 commissioning test drives: Commencing of test drives occurred on 25.03.1999.
M2 commissioning stage 1 section: Stage 1 commissioning between Taksim and Levent occurred on 16.09.2000.
M2 commissioning stage 1 extension: Stage 1 commissioning between Levent and 4. Levent occurred on 31.10.2000.
M2 commissioning stage 2 section: Stage 2 commissioning of Taksim - Şişhane and 4. Levent - Atatürk Oto Sanayi Section occurred on 30.01.2009.
M2 commissioning stage 2 station: Commissioning at Darüşşafaka Station occurred on 02.09.2010.
M2 commissioning stage 2 branch: Commissioning at Seyrantepe Station occurred on 11.11.2010.
M2 commissioning stage 2 terminus: Commissioning at Hacıosman Station occurred on 09.07.2011.
M2 commissioning stage 3 extension: Stage 3 commissioning of Yenikapı Extension occurred on 15.02.2014.
M2 commissioning stage 3 station: Commissioning at Vezneciler - Istanbul University Station occurred on 16.03.2014.
M2 transfer Yenikapı Station: Start terminus Yenikapı Station offers transfer connections to M1A Yenikapı-Atatürk Airport Metro Line, M1B Yenikapı-Kirazlı Metro Line, T6 Sirkeci-Kazlıçeşme Tram Line, and Marmaray.
M2 transfer Vezneciler - Istanbul University Station: Vezneciler - Istanbul University Station offers transfer connections to T1 Kabataş-Bağcılar Tram Line.
M2 transfer Haliç Station: Haliç Station offers transfer connections to T5 Eminönü-Alibeyköy Tram Line.
M2 transfer Şişhane Station: Şişhane Station offers transfer connections to T2 İstiklal Caddesi Tram Line and F2 Karaköy-Beyoğlu Historical Tunnel Line.
M2 transfer Taksim Station: Taksim Station offers transfer connections to T2 İstiklal Caddesi Tram Line and F1 Taksim-Kabataş Funicular Line.
M2 transfer Şişli - Mecidiyeköy Station: Şişli - Mecidiyeköy Station offers transfer connections to M7 Yıldız-Mahmutbey Metro Line and Metrobus (BRT).
M2 transfer Gayrettepe Station: Gayrettepe Station offers transfer connections to M11 Gayrettepe-Istanbul Airport-Halkalı Metro Line and Metrobus (BRT).
M2 transfer Levent Station: Levent Station offers transfer connections to M6 Levent-Boğaziçi Ü./Hisarüstü Metro Line.
M2 transfer Sanayi Mahallesi Station: Sanayi Mahallesi Station offers an internal transfer connection to the Seyrantepe Shuttle Extension via a separate platform area without leaving the system.
