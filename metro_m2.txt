M2 document: Metro Istanbul line M2, Yenikapı - Seyrantepe - Hacıosman Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M2 overview: Line Identifier | M2 Yenikapı - Seyrantepe - Hacıosman Metro Line.
M2 overview: Description | Construction for the M2 line began in 1992. The first operational stage was commissioned on September 16th, 2000. The mainline serves the Yenikapı to Hacıosman route, while a dedicated branch line operates from Sanayi Mahallesi Station to the Seyrantepe district. Passengers traveling to Seyrantepe must transfer at Sanayi Mahallesi Station.

M2 metric: Mainline Frequency (Yenikapı-Hacıosman) | Every 3 min. 55 sec. (8-car operation on weekdays/Saturdays).
M2 metric: Branch Frequency (Sanayi Mahallesi-Seyrantepe) | Every 8.5 min. (all day).
M2 line: The M2 mainline (Yenikapı to Hacıosman) spans 15 stations: 01-Yenikapı (Start Terminus), 02-Vezneciler - İstanbul Üniversitesi, 03-Haliç, 04-Şişhane, 05-Taksim, 06-Osmanbey, 07-Şişli - Mecidiyeköy, 08-Gayrettepe, 09-Levent, 10-4.Levent, 11-Sanayi Mahallesi (transfer required for Seyrantepe branch), 12-İTÜ - Ayazağa, 13-Atatürk Oto Sanayi, 14-Darüşşafaka, and 15-Hacıosman (End Terminus).
M2 branch station: Seyrantepe (accessible via shuttle transfer at Sanayi Mahallesi).
M2 station structure: Bridge / Viaduct Station (1) | Haliç Station (built over the Haliç Metro Bridge).
M2 station structure: Tunnel / Underground Stations (15) | All other remaining stations are built as underground/tunnel stations.
