M8 overview: Line Identifier | M8 Bostancı - Parseller Metro Line.
M8 overview: Operator | Metro Istanbul.
M8 overview: Description | Construction of the M8 line was initiated by IMM in February 2016. It was planned and constructed to connect the major east-west metro and rail lines on the Anatolian side of Istanbul in a north-south direction.

M8 metric: Daily Passengers / Ridership | 85,000 passengers.
M8 metric: Daily Trips | 240 journeys (two-way total).
M8 metric: Travel Frequency | 7 minutes.
M8 line full station list: The M8 line (Bostancı to Parseller) spans 13 stations: 01-Bostancı (Start Terminus), 02-Emin Ali Paşa, 03-Ayşekadın, 04-Kozyatağı, 05-Küçükbakkalköy, 06-İçerenköy, 07-Kayışdağı, 08-Mevlana, 09-İMES, 10-MODOKO-KEYAP, 11-Dudullu, 12-Huzur, and 13-Parseller (End Terminus).
M8 station structure: All 13 stations on the M8 line are built completely underground.
