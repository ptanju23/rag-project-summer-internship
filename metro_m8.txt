M8 document: Metro Istanbul line M8, Bostancı - Parseller Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M8 line identifier: M8 Bostancı - Parseller Metro Line.
M8 operator: The operator of the M8 line is Metro Istanbul.
M8 description: Construction of the M8 line was initiated by IMM in February 2016.
M8 description: It was planned and constructed to connect the major east-west metro and rail lines on the Anatolian side of Istanbul in a north-south direction.
M8 line length metric: Total line length is 14.27 km.
M8 total stations metric: Total stations on M8 route is 13 stations.
M8 fleet size metric: Active fleet size is 40 cars running as 10 4-car train sets.
M8 travel duration metric: One-way journey duration is 26 minutes.
M8 operating hours metric: Operating hours are 06:00-00:00.
M8 passenger capacity metric: Daily passengers or ridership is 85,000 passengers.
M8 daily journeys metric: Daily trips total 240 journeys for two-way total.
M8 frequency metric: Travel frequency is 7 minutes.
M8 full station list: The M8 line spans 13 stations from start terminus 01-Bostancı, 02-Emin Ali Paşa, 03-Ayşekadın, 04-Kozyatağı, 05-Küçükbakkalköy, 06-İçerenköy, 07-Kayışdağı, 08-Mevlana, 09-İMES, 10-MODOKO-KEYAP, 11-Dudullu, 12-Huzur, to end terminus 13-Parseller.
M8 station structure: All 13 stations on the M8 line are built completely underground.
M8 accessibility features: Line is equipped with 160 escalators and 51 elevators.
M8 commissioning construction start: Construction start date by IMM occurred in February 2016.
M8 commissioning opening: Revenue opening date occurred on 06.01.2023.
M8 transfer Bostancı Station: Start terminus Bostancı Station offers transfer connections to B1 Marmaray Line, High-Speed Train (YHT) Station, sea lines, and sea buses.
M8 transfer Kozyatağı Station: Kozyatağı Station offers transfer connections to M4 Kadıköy - Sabiha Gökçen Airport Metro Line.
M8 transfer Dudullu Station: Dudullu Station offers transfer connections to M5 Üsküdar - Samandıra Metro Line.
