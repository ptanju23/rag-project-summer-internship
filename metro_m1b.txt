M1B document: Metro Istanbul line M1B, Yenikapı - Kirazlı Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M1B line identifier: M1B Yenikapı - Kirazlı Metro Line.
M1B operator: The operator of the M1B line is Metro Istanbul.
M1B description: M1B shares common tracks with M1A between Yenikapı and Otogar, then branches off westward through Esenler and Bağcılar Square, terminating at Kirazlı where it integrates with the M3 line.
M1B network length metric: Total network length is 26.8 km for the combined M1A/M1B network total.
M1B total stations metric: Total stations on M1B route is 13 stations.
M1B fleet size metric: Active fleet size is 105 vehicles.
M1B travel duration metric: One-way journey duration is 35 minutes.
M1B operating hours metric: Operating hours are 06:00-00:00.
M1B passenger capacity metric: Daily passenger capacity is 400,000 passengers.
M1B frequency metric: Peak hour frequency is every 2.5 minutes.
M1B full station list: The M1B line spans 13 stations from start terminus 01-Yenikapı, 02-Aksaray, 03-Emniyet-Fatih, 04-Topkapı-Ulubatlı, 05-Bayrampaşa-Maltepe, 06-Sağmalcılar, 07-Kocatepe, 08-Otogar (Coach Station), 09-Esenler, 10-Menderes, 11-Üçyüzlü, 12-Bağcılar Meydan, to end terminus 13-Kirazlı / Kirazlı-Bağcılar.
M1B station structure seating: All stations feature indoor seating areas.
M1B accessibility features: Accessibility infrastructure for M1B features 96 escalators and 41 elevators.
M1B tunnel underground stations: Yenikapı, Aksaray, Emniyet-Fatih, Topkapı-Ulubatlı, Menderes, Üçyüzlü, Bağcılar Square, and Kirazlı Bağcılar are tunnel and underground stations.
M1B at-grade ground stations: Bayrampaşa-Maltepe, Sağmalcılar, Kartaltepe-Kocatepe, Otogar, and Esenler are at-grade ground stations.
M1B commissioning stage 1 section: Stage 1 from Emniyet-Fatih to Kartaltepe-Kocatepe was commissioned on 11.03.1989.
M1B commissioning stage 1 Aksaray: Aksaray Station was commissioned on 03.09.1989.
M1B commissioning stage 4 extension: Otogar - Kirazlı Extension & M1B Operation Commissioning occurred on 14.06.2013.
M1B commissioning stage 4 integration: Yenikapı Station Integration occurred on 09.11.2014.
M1B transfer Yenikapı Station: Start terminus Yenikapı Station offers transfer connections to M1A Metro Line, M2 Hacıosman Metro Line, T6 Sirkeci-Kazlıçeşme Tram Line, and Marmaray.
M1B transfer Aksaray Station: Aksaray Station offers transfer connections to T1 Kabataş-Bağcılar Tram Line.
M1B transfer Topkapı-Ulubatlı Station: Topkapı-Ulubatlı Station offers transfer connections to T4 Topkapı-Mescid-i Selam Tram Line.
M1B transfer Bağcılar Meydan Station: Bağcılar Meydan Station offers transfer connections to T1 Kabataş-Bağcılar Tram Line.
M1B transfer Kirazlı Station: End terminus Kirazlı Station offers transfer connections to M3 Bakırköy-Kayaşehir Metro Line.
