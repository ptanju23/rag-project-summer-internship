M1B document: Metro Istanbul line M1B, Yenikapı - Kirazlı Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M1B overview: Line Identifier | M1B Yenikapı - Kirazlı Metro Line.
M1B overview: Operator | Metro Istanbul.
M1B overview: Description | M1B shares common tracks with M1A between Yenikapı and Otogar. After Otogar Station, the line branches off westward, traveling through Esenler and Bağcılar Square, and terminates at Kirazlı, where it integrates with the M3 line.
