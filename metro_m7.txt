M7 document: Metro Istanbul line M7, Yıldız - Mahmutbey Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M7 line identifier: M7 Yıldız - Mahmutbey Metro Line.
M7 operator: The operator of the M7 line is Metro Istanbul.
M7 description: The M7 line is the first fully automatic driverless metro on the European side of Istanbul, designed to extend between Kabataş and Esenyurt upon full completion.
M7 description: The current operational route connects Beşiktaş to Mahmutbey through Şişli, Kağıthane, Eyüpsultan, Gaziosmanpaşa, Esenler, and Bağcılar districts.
M7 line length metric: Total line length is 20 km.
M7 total stations metric: Total stations on M7 route is 17 stations.
M7 fleet size metric: Active fleet size is 80 vehicles.
M7 travel duration metric: One-way travel time is 36 minutes.
M7 operating hours metric: Operating hours are 06:00-00:00.
M7 vehicle capacity metric: Vehicle passenger capacity is 2,160 passengers per train set.
M7 transport capacity metric: Hourly transport capacity is 70,000 passengers per hour per direction.
M7 daily journeys metric: Daily trips total 176 one-way runs.
M7 frequency metric: Peak hour travel frequency is every 4 minutes.
M7 full station list: The M7 line spans 17 stations from start terminus 01-Yıldız, 02-Fulya, 03-Mecidiyeköy, 04-Çağlayan, 05-Kağıthane (located on a viaduct), 06-Nurtepe, 07-Alibeyköy (located on a viaduct), 08-Çırçır Mahallesi, 09-Veysel Karani-Akşemsettin, 10-Yeşilpınar, 11-Kazım Karabekir, 12-Yenimahalle, 13-Karadeniz Mahallesi, 14-Giyimkent-Tekstilkent, 15-Oruç Reis, 16-Göztepe Mahallesi, to end terminus 17-Mahmutbey.
M7 station structure underground: 15 stations are completely underground.
M7 station structure viaduct: 2 stations are elevated on viaducts including Kağıthane and Alibeyköy.
M7 accessibility features: Infrastructure features tactile embossed floor guides, disabled restrooms, 225 escalators, 108 elevators, and 1,192 CCTV cameras.
M7 vehicle accessibility: Vehicles feature an Induction Loop System broadcasting audio specifically for hearing aids.
M7 platform safety: Equipped with fully domestic production Platform Screen Door Systems (PAKS) reducing costs by 50% compared to imported equivalents.
M7 ranking metrics: Ranks 9th in Europe and 21st in the World for line length, 24th in the World for number of stations, 7th in the World for automated driverless operation line length combined with M5, and 1st in the World for instant train capacity at 2,160 passengers.
M7 commissioning stage 1: Stage 1 commissioning of Mecidiyeköy - Mahmutbey occurred on 28.10.2020.
M7 commissioning stage 2: Stage 2 commissioning of Yıldız - Mecidiyeköy opening Fulya and Yıldız Stations occurred on 02.01.2023.
M7 transfer Mecidiyeköy Station: Mecidiyeköy Station offers transfer connections to M2 Yenikapı - Hacıosman Metro Line and Metrobus (BRT) Line.
M7 transfer Kağıthane Station: Kağıthane Station offers transfer connections to M11 Gayrettepe - Istanbul Airport - Halkalı Metro Line.
M7 transfer Alibeyköy Station: Alibeyköy Station offers transfer connections to T5 Eminönü - Alibeyköy Tram Line.
M7 transfer Karadeniz Mahallesi Station: Karadeniz Mahallesi Station offers transfer connections to T4 Topkapı - Mescid-i Selam Tram Line.
M7 transfer Mahmutbey Station: End terminus Mahmutbey Station offers transfer connections to M3 Bakırköy - Kayaşehir Metro Line.
