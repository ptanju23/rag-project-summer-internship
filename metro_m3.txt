M3 document: Metro Istanbul line M3, Bakırköy Sahil - Kayaşehir Merkez Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M3 line identifier: M3 Bakırköy Sahil - Kayaşehir Merkez Metro Line.
M3 description: The M3 line spans 26.7 km connecting Bakırköy Sahil to Kayaşehir Merkez through 20 stations, with its first phase inaugurated in 2013.
M3 description: A shuttle service originally operated between İkitelli Sanayi and Olimpiyat before being transferred to M9; extensions to Kayaşehir Merkez in 2023 and the southern extension in March 2024 established full service from Bakırköy Sahil to Kayaşehir Merkez.
M3 line length metric: Total line length is 26.7 km.
M3 total stations metric: Total stations on M3 route is 20 stations.
M3 platform length metric: Platform length is 180 meters supporting 8-car train operations.
M3 frequency metric: Headway at peak hours is 7 minutes.
M3 depot location metric: Depot & Workshop Location is Olympic Campus featuring 70,000 m² total area and 10,000 m² enclosed space.
M3 depot fleet capacity metric: Depot fleet capacity is 120 vehicles.
M3 full station list: The M3 line spans 20 stations from start terminus 01-Bakırköy Sahil, 02-Özgürlük Meydanı, 03-İncirli, 04-Haznedar, 05-İlkyuva, 06-Yıldıztepe, 07-Molla Gürani, 08-Kirazlı-Bağcılar, 09-Yenimahalle, 10-Mahmutbey (includes an additional 3rd track accessing vehicle yard), 11-İSTOÇ, 12-İkitelli Sanayi, 13-Turgut Özal, 14-Siteler, 15-Başak Konutları, 16-Başakşehir-Metrokent, 17-Onurkent, 18-Şehir Hastanesi, 19-Toplu Konutlar, to end terminus 20-Kayaşehir Merkez.
M3 station structure: All 20 stations are built as tunnel and underground stations.
M3 engineering features: System features fully automatic signaling, switch, and vehicle systems with manual override capability.
M3 safety features: Safety infrastructure includes an interactive, self-adjusting fire safety system constructed with heat-resistant, non-toxic materials, smoke control, and tested evacuation protocols.
M3 security features: Security includes 24/7 CCTV camera monitoring, simulation-tested emergency response protocols, and uniformed security personnel.
M3 commissioning phase 1: Phase 1 opening of Kirazlı - Başakşehir Metrokent Line occurred on 14.06.2013.
M3 commissioning Olimpiyat: Opening of Olimpiyat Station occurred on 22.11.2013 (later integrated into M9).
M3 commissioning phase 2: Phase 2 opening of Onurkent, Şehir Hastanesi, Toplu Konutlar, and Kayaşehir Merkez Stations occurred on 08.04.2023.
M3 commissioning phase 3: Phase 3 opening of the Southern Extension to Bakırköy Sahil occurred on 10.03.2024.
M3 commissioning station: Opening of Yıldıztepe Station occurred on 08.10.2025.
M3 transfer Kayaşehir Merkez Station: End terminus Kayaşehir Merkez Station offers transfer connections to M11 Gayrettepe - Istanbul Airport - Halkalı Line.
M3 transfer İkitelli Sanayi Station: İkitelli Sanayi Station offers transfer connections to M9 Ataköy - Olimpiyat Line.
M3 transfer Mahmutbey Station: Mahmutbey Station offers transfer connections to M7 Yıldız - Mahmutbey Line.
M3 transfer Kirazlı-Bağcılar Station: Kirazlı-Bağcılar Station offers transfer connections to M1B Yenikapı - Kirazlı Line.
M3 transfer İncirli Station: İncirli Station offers transfer connections to M1A Yenikapı - Atatürk Airport Line and Metrobus (BRT) Line.
M3 transfer Özgürlük Meydanı Station: Özgürlük Meydanı Station offers transfer connections to B1 Halkalı - Gebze Marmaray Line and Bakırköy High-Speed Train Station.
