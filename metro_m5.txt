M5 document: Metro Istanbul line M5, Üsküdar - Sultanbeyli Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M5 overview: Line Identifier | M5 Üsküdar - Sultanbeyli Metro Line.
M5 overview: Operator | Metro Istanbul.
M5 overview: Description | The M5 line is the second metro line on the Anatolian side of Istanbul and the first fully automated driverless (UTO) metro line in Türkiye. Starting at Üsküdar Square, it traverses Ümraniye and Çekmeköy, extends through Sancaktepe, and terminates at Sultanbeyli. Future extensions to the Kurtköy area are currently under design and planning.
M5 metric: Total Line Length | 30.9 km.
M5 metric: Total Stations on M5 Route | 24 stations.
M5 metric: Active Fleet Size | 142 vehicles.
M5 metric: One-Way Journey Duration | 50 minutes.
M5 metric: Weekday Operating Hours | 06:00-24:00.
M5 metric: Weekend Operating Hours | 66-hour continuous Night Metro service (06:00 Friday to 00:00 Sunday).
M5 metric: Daily Passengers / Ridership | 375,000 passengers.
M5 metric: Daily Number of Trips | 354 trips (both directions combined).
M5 metric: Peak Hour Travel Frequency | Every 5 minutes.
M5 line full station list: The M5 line (Üsküdar to Sultanbeyli) spans 24 stations: 01-Üsküdar (Start Terminus; platform length: 150 meters), 02-Fıstıkağacı, 03-Bağlarbaşı, 04-Altunizade, 05-Kısıklı (entrance-to-concourse level access exclusively via elevator), 06-Bulgurlu, 07-Ümraniye, 08-Çarşı, 09-Yamanevler, 10-Çakmak, 11-Ihlamurkuyu, 12-Altınşehir, 13-İmam Hatip Lisesi, 14-Dudullu, 15-Necip Fazıl, 16-Çekmeköy, 17-Meclis, 18-Sarıgazi, 19-Sancaktepe Şehir Hastanesi, 20-Sancaktepe, 21-Samandıra Merkez, 22-Veysel Karani, 23-Hasanpaşa, and 24-Sultanbeyli (End Terminus).
M5 engineering: Construction Methods | Tunnels excavated using TBM (Tunnel Boring Machine) and NATM (New Austrian Tunnelling Method). All 24 stations are built underground.
M5 engineering: Platform Specifications | Station platforms are 140 meters long (150 meters at Üsküdar Station), engineered for 6-car train operations.
M5 automation: Automation & Platform Safety | Operates on an Unattended Train Operation (UTO) driverless system. All platforms are equipped with Platform Screen Doors (PSD) for passenger safety.
M5 accessibility: Stations feature dedicated accessibility turnstiles, elevators, and escalators. At Kısıklı Station, concourse access is exclusively provided via elevators.
