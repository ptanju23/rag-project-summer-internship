M5 document: Metro Istanbul line M5, Üsküdar - Sultanbeyli Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M5 line identifier: M5 Üsküdar - Sultanbeyli Metro Line.
M5 operator: The operator of the M5 line is Metro Istanbul.
M5 description: The M5 line is the second metro line on the Anatolian side of Istanbul and the first fully automated driverless (UTO) metro line in Türkiye.
M5 description: Starting at Üsküdar Square, it traverses Ümraniye and Çekmeköy, extends through Sancaktepe, and terminates at Sultanbeyli, with future extensions to Kurtköy under planning.
M5 line length metric: Total line length is 30.9 km.
M5 total stations metric: Total stations on M5 route is 24 stations.
M5 fleet size metric: Active fleet size is 142 vehicles.
M5 travel duration metric: One-way journey duration is 50 minutes.
M5 operating hours metric: Weekday operating hours are 06:00-24:00.
M5 weekend operating hours metric: Weekend operating hours feature a 66-hour continuous Night Metro service from 06:00 Friday to 00:00 Sunday.
M5 passenger capacity metric: Daily passengers or ridership is 375,000 passengers.
M5 daily journeys metric: Daily number of trips is 354 trips with both directions combined.
M5 frequency metric: Peak hour travel frequency is every 5 minutes.
M5 full station list: The M5 line spans 24 stations from start terminus 01-Üsküdar (platform length: 150 meters), 02-Fıstıkağacı, 03-Bağlarbaşı, 04-Altunizade, 05-Kısıklı (entrance-to-concourse level access exclusively via elevator), 06-Bulgurlu, 07-Ümraniye, 08-Çarşı, 09-Yamanevler, 10-Çakmak, 11-Ihlamurkuyu, 12-Altınşehir, 13-İmam Hatip Lisesi, 14-Dudullu, 15-Necip Fazıl, 16-Çekmeköy, 17-Meclis, 18-Sarıgazi, 19-Sancaktepe Şehir Hastanesi, 20-Sancaktepe, 21-Samandıra Merkez, 22-Veysel Karani, 23-Hasanpaşa, to end terminus 24-Sultanbeyli.
M5 engineering construction: Tunnels were excavated using TBM (Tunnel Boring Machine) and NATM (New Austrian Tunnelling Method).
M5 engineering structure: All 24 stations are built underground.
M5 platform specifications: Station platforms are 140 meters long (150 meters at Üsküdar Station), engineered for 6-car train operations.
M5 automation system: Operates on an Unattended Train Operation (UTO) driverless system with Platform Screen Doors (PSD) equipped at all platforms.
M5 accessibility features: Stations feature dedicated accessibility turnstiles, elevators, and escalators, with Kısıklı Station concourse access provided exclusively via elevators.
M5 commissioning phase 1: Phase 1 commissioning between Üsküdar and Yamanevler occurred on 15.12.2017.
M5 commissioning phase 2: Phase 2 commissioning between Yamanevler and Çekmeköy occurred on 21.10.2018.
M5 commissioning phase 3: Phase 3 commissioning between Çekmeköy and Samandıra Merkez occurred on 16.03.2024.
M5 commissioning phase 4: Phase 4 commissioning between Samandıra Merkez and Sultanbeyli Merkez occurred on 22.05.2026.
M5 transfer Üsküdar Station: Start terminus Üsküdar Station offers transfer connections to Marmaray, İETT bus hub, and ferry piers (maritime lines).
M5 transfer Altunizade Station: Altunizade Station offers transfer connections to Metrobus (BRT) Line.
M5 transfer Dudullu Station: Dudullu Station offers transfer connections to M8 Bostancı - Parseller Metro Line.
M5 planned transfer Çarşı Station: Çarşı Station features a planned transfer connection to M12 Göztepe - Ümraniye Metro Line.
M5 planned transfer Altunizade Station: Altunizade Station features a planned transfer connection to M14 Altunizade - Bosna Boulevard Metro Line.
