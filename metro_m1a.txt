M1A document: Metro Istanbul line M1A, Yenikapı - Atatürk Airport Metro Line Technical & Operational Documentation.
M1A line identifier: M1A Yenikapı - Atatürk Airport Metro Line.
M1A operator: The operator of the M1A line is Metro Istanbul.
M1A description: M1A is the first rail system operation of Metro Istanbul, connecting Suriçi to Bakırköy and extending from Yenikapı through Bayrampaşa, Otogar, Merter, and Zeytinburnu to Atatürk Airport.
M1A shared network: The section between Yenikapı Station and Otogar Station is operated jointly with the M1B Line.
M1A network length metric: Total network length is 26.8 km for the combined M1A/M1B network total.
M1A total stations metric: Total stations on M1A route is 18 stations.
M1A fleet size metric: Active fleet size is 105 vehicles.
M1A travel duration metric: One-way journey duration is 25 minutes.
M1A operating hours metric: Operating hours are 06:00-00:00.
M1A passenger capacity metric: Daily passenger capacity is 400,000 passengers.
M1A daily journeys metric: Daily journeys total 168 one-way journeys on the Yenikapı - Kirazlı joint track.
M1A frequency metric: Peak hour frequency is every 4 minutes.
M1A full station list: The M1A line spans 18 stations from start terminus 01-Yenikapı, 02-Aksaray, 03-Emniyet-Fatih, 04-Topkapı-Ulubatlı, 05-Bayrampaşa-Maltepe, 06-Sağmalcılar, 07-Kocatepe (Kartaltepe-Kocatepe), 08-Otogar, 09-Terazidere, 10-Davutpaşa YTÜ, 11-Merter, 12-Zeytinburnu, 13-Bakırköy-İncirli, 14-Bahçelievler, 15-Ataköy-Şirinevler, 16-Yenibosna, 17-DTM-İstanbul Fuar Merkezi, to terminal station 18-Atatürk Havalimanı.
M1A station structure seating: All stations feature indoor seating areas.
M1A accessibility features: Accessibility infrastructure includes 61 escalators and 45 elevators.
M1A tunnel underground stations: Yenikapı, Aksaray, Emniyet-Fatih, Topkapı-Ulubatlı, Bakırköy-İncirli, Bahçelievler, and Atatürk Airport are tunnel and underground stations.
M1A viaduct stations: Davutpaşa, Merter, and DTM-İstanbul Fair Centre are viaduct stations.
M1A at-grade ground stations: Bayrampaşa-Maltepe, Sağmalcılar, Kartaltepe-Kocatepe, Otogar, Terazidere, Zeytinburnu, Ataköy-Şirinevler, and Yenibosna are at-grade ground stations.
M1A commissioning stage 1 section: Stage 1 from Emniyet-Fatih to Kartaltepe-Kocatepe was commissioned on 11.03.1989.
M1A commissioning stage 1 Aksaray: Aksaray Station was commissioned on 03.09.1989.
M1A commissioning stage 2 section: Stage 2 from Kartaltepe-Kocatepe to Zeytinburnu was commissioned on 31.01.1994.
M1A commissioning stage 2 Bakırköy: Section from Zeytinburnu to Bakırköy was commissioned on 07.03.1994.
M1A commissioning stage 3 Ataköy: Section from Bakırköy to Ataköy was commissioned on 26.07.1995.
M1A commissioning stage 3 Yenibosna: Section from Ataköy to Yenibosna was commissioned on 25.08.1995.
M1A commissioning stage 3 Bahçelievler: Bahçelievler Station was commissioned on 15.01.1999.
M1A commissioning stage 3 airport: DTM-İstanbul Fair Centre and Atatürk Airport stations were commissioned on 20.12.2002.
M1A commissioning stage 4 extension: Otogar - Kirazlı Extension and M1B Operation were commissioned on 14.06.2013.
M1A commissioning stage 4 integration: Yenikapı Station Integration was commissioned on 09.11.2014.
M1A transfer Yenikapı Station: Starting station Yenikapı Station offers transfer connections to M1B Metro Line, M2 Hacıosman Metro Line, T6 Sirkeci-Kazlıçeşme Tram Line, and Marmaray.
M1A transfer Aksaray Station: Aksaray Station offers transfer connections to T1 Kabataş-Bağcılar Tram Line.
M1A transfer Merter Station: Merter Station offers transfer connections to Metrobus (BRT).
M1A transfer Zeytinburnu Station: Zeytinburnu Station offers transfer connections to T1 Kabataş-Bağcılar Tram Line and Metrobus (BRT).
M1A transfer İncirli Station: İncirli Station offers transfer connections to M3 Bakırköy-Kayaşehir Metro Line and Metrobus (BRT).
M1A transfer Bahçelievler Station: Bahçelievler Station offers transfer connections to Metrobus (BRT).
M1A transfer Ataköy-Şirinevler Station: Ataköy-Şirinevler Station offers transfer connections to Metrobus (BRT).
M1A transfer Yenibosna Station: Yenibosna Station offers transfer connections to M9 Ataköy-Olimpiyat Metro Line and Metrobus (BRT).
